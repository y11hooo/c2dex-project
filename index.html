Abstract

High-quality dexterous-hand data are scarce and expensive to collect, whereas monocular human videos provide a scalable source of diverse manipulation demonstrations. However, transferring such videos to dexterous robots remains challenging because monocular hand–object interaction (HOI) reconstruction often produces temporally unstable contacts, while conventional retargeting methods fail to preserve task-relevant contacts and local interaction geometry across different hand embodiments. We present C2Dex, a video-to-dexterity learning framework that combines contact-consistent HOI reconstruction with interaction-preserving retargeting. C2Dex aggregates noisy frame-wise contact observations in the canonical object space to recover stable object-side contacts, which guide trajectory optimization toward temporally coherent and physically plausible HOI trajectories. It then explicitly transfers these contacts to the target dexterous hand and applies Laplacian interaction optimization to preserve the surrounding local hand–object geometry. The generated trajectories can further serve as references for residual policy learning to compensate for execution errors. Experiments on DexYCB and TACO show that C2Dex achieves end-to-end trajectory success rates of 55.56% and 26.67%, respectively, while reducing simulation displacement from 4.55 cm to 1.57 cm and improving retargeting accuracy. Real-robot experiments further demonstrate the physical executability of the generated trajectories across diverse contact-rich manipulation tasks.

Method

Given a monocular human video, C2Dex first performs contact-consistent HOI reconstruction by aggregating frame-wise contact observations into stable object-side contacts, and then transfers the reconstructed interaction to a dexterous hand through contact-interaction-preserving retargeting with stable-contact preservation, Laplacian interaction optimization, and residual reinforcement learning.

Stable Contact Map

Frame-wise contact candidates from silhouette overlap and ray casting are filtered by surface-normal compatibility, then transformed into the canonical object frame and clustered within locally stable temporal segments.

HOI Optimization

The recovered stable contacts guide trajectory-level optimization with contact guidance, smoothness, and collision handling, yielding temporally coherent and penetration-free hand–object trajectories.

Interaction-Preserving Retargeting

Laplacian interaction optimization matches local interaction coordinates rather than absolute keypoints, while stable-contact optimization transfers task-relevant contacts to the target embodiment under joint limits and SDF collision constraints.

Residual Policy Learning

The optimized trajectory serves as a motion reference for a residual PPO policy rewarded for object tracking, hand tracking, contact consistency, and smoothness, compensating for remaining dynamics errors.
